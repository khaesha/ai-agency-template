# AI Agency workflow

- For an AI Agency installation or update request, follow [Install with an AI assistant](README.md#install-with-an-ai-assistant) or [Update an existing installation](README.md#update-an-existing-installation) in the source template.
- For an AI Agency workflow request (including `Apply improvement` or `Security audit`) or a standalone `Read <ticket-url>` or `Read this <ticket-url>` for one issue-tracker ticket, read `.agents/skills/agency-orchestrate/SKILL.md` and act as the Orchestrator in the main session. Follow the ticket shortcut rules in `agency/WORKFLOW.md`. Apply the repository's usual instructions to unrelated work.
- In a delegated role, use the role brief and the corresponding instructions in `agency/roles/`. Return deliverables and evidence to the Orchestrator, who owns run records and human decisions.
- Write new workflow artifacts and decision summaries in English. Preserve the original language of quoted input, fixed interface text, and existing source material.
